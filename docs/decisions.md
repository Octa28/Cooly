# Design decisions

Format: CLAUDE.md section 14.4. Teammates mark entries **accepted**; the "System design" slides use accepted entries.

| ID | Date | Decision | Principle(s) | Alternatives considered | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | 2026-11-25 | The **Recipes** screen (`/recipes`) is search-first: you add ingredients in a search field at the top, and the recipe lists below update as you add. Screens 05 (Add ingredients) and 06 (Recipe results) are merged into one screen. | Nielsen #1 Visibility of system status (results change as you add); Hick's law (one field, one job); UD 6 Low physical effort (no screen switch) | Separate Add and Results screens, as in the original screen list | Octavian's frontend notes; text wireframe approved in chat | accepted (Octavian) |
| D-02 | 2026-11-25 | Only motion: when the lists reorder, cards slide to their new place in 200 ms (ease-out). No staggered fade-ins or parallax on scroll. | CLAUDE.md 11.2 and 11.8; reduced motion respected | Scroll-reveal and parallax, as asked for in the notes ("very visual and dynamic scroll") | The notes conflicted with CLAUDE.md; Octavian chose this | accepted (Octavian) |
| D-03 | 2026-11-25 | The fridge and the chosen diet are stored in this browser (`localStorage`) until Supabase is set up. | Works without an account (NFR-03); privacy: nothing leaves the phone | Supabase anonymous sign-in now (needs a project and keys first) | — | proposed |
| D-04 | 2026-11-25 | **Halal** tag = no pork and no meat at all, because we can't know if meat is halal-certified. Fish, eggs and dairy are allowed. | Safety first for a hard filter (9.3) | Tag chicken recipes as halal with a "buy halal chicken" note | — | proposed: check with halal teammates or users |
| D-05 | 2026-11-25 | Picking an ingredient adds a typical pack amount (e.g. eggs ×6, pasta 500 g). Exact amounts will be editable on the Fridge screen. | Nielsen #7 Flexibility & efficiency (fast adding); Tesler's law | Ask for a quantity on every add | Recommendation approved in chat | accepted (Octavian) |
| D-06 | 2026-11-25 | Staples (salt, pepper, oil) count as available and are left out of "You have 5 of 7". | Nielsen #2 Match with the real world; Miller (fewer things to read) | Count them as ingredients you have | — | proposed |
| D-07 | 2026-11-25 | Until there are prices, each list is sorted by the share of the recipe you already have, then by cooking time. Cost to complete goes first once `data/offers.json` exists. | Scrutability ("You have 5 of 7") | Popularity order | CLAUDE.md 9.4 puts cost first | reverted: with example prices, Best match now sorts by cost first (D-12) |
| D-08 | 2026-11-25 | Recipe cards are text-only, with a thin bar showing how much of the recipe you have. | CLAUDE.md 11.7 (no fake food photos) | Stock photos, emoji | — | proposed |
| D-09 | 2026-11-25 | Fridge, Shopping list and Settings tabs show an honest "not built yet" page instead of being hidden. | Nielsen #4 Consistency (the tab bar stays the same as it grows) | Hide unfinished tabs | — | proposed |
| D-10 | 2026-11-25 | Kept the defaults of the Next.js 16 template: Cache Components, Partial Prefetching, Tailwind v4. Tailwind v4 has no `tailwind.config`; tokens are mapped in `app/globals.css` with `@theme`. | CLAUDE.md 7 (stack) and 11.3 (tokens as CSS variables) | Turning these features off | `npm run build` passes | proposed |
| D-11 | 2026-11-25 | Filters open from a button under the search bar with a ☰ (hamburger) icon and the word "Filters", into a bottom sheet with diet, allergies and equipment. The diet chips moved off the main screen into the sheet. | Octavian's request; Hick's law (fewer controls on the main screen); UD 3 (the word "Filters" says what it does) | A funnel or sliders icon, which matches filter conventions better (Nielsen #4); ☰ usually means site navigation | Octavian's notes | accepted (Octavian), icon open to change |
| D-12 | 2026-11-25 | Sort options: Best match (default), Price, Cooking time, Uses most of your fridge, Fewest ingredients. They sort **inside** each of the three lists; the lists keep their fixed order. | Price and time asked for by Octavian; "Uses most of your fridge" supports the less-food-waste goal; Hick's law (5 options) | One flat list sorted by the option | — | proposed (the last two options) |
| D-13 | 2026-11-25 | Price slider = cost to finish a recipe (what you still have to buy), with two handles and From/To fields. The top end is the most expensive cost to finish, rounded up to €0.50, and dragging to the end means "no upper limit". Recipes outside the range go behind a "Show N recipes outside €X – €Y" button. | Nielsen #1 (nothing hidden silently, CLAUDE.md 9.5); UD 2 (drag or type) | Max budget only (CLAUDE.md screen 10) | Octavian's notes | accepted (Octavian) |
| D-14 | 2026-11-25 | A "From" price above €0 also hides ready-to-cook recipes (they cost €0 to finish). The summary then says "in this price range". | Consistency (Nielsen #4): the range means the same for every recipe | Always show ready recipes, whatever the From price | — | proposed: check in user tests |
| D-15 | 2026-11-25 | Until the team collects real offers, `data/offers.json` holds **invented example prices** for Albert Heijn and Jumbo, marked `"example": true`. The UI says "Example prices, not checked in a store yet" wherever prices appear. | CLAUDE.md 3 and 9.5 (never present invented prices as real) | No prices at all, so no price sort or slider | — | proposed (temporary) |
